About Us

We write about the spaces that shape us.

Aurex Built Perspectives is an independent editorial publication dedicated to architecture, interior design, and the built environment. We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and critical essays that help readers create spaces of lasting value.

Our Mission

We believe that good design is not a luxury but a necessity. It shapes how we live, work, and feel. Our mission is to demystify the design process, celebrate material honesty, and champion a slower, more thoughtful approach to building and decorating.

In a world saturated with fleeting trends, we focus on principles that endure: proportion, light, texture, and the quiet intelligence of restraint. We don't chase novelty for its own sake; we look for ideas that stand the test of time.

“Architecture is the thoughtful making of space.” — Louis Kahn
Coverage

What We Cover

Our editorial spans the full spectrum of the built environment, from the scale of a city to the grain of a material. We publish in-depth articles, interviews, and guides across these core areas:

  • Interior Design — spatial composition, colour, texture, and the psychology of home.
  • Architecture — residential, cultural, and adaptive reuse projects, with a focus on climate response.
  • Materials — from lime plaster and clay paint to engineered timber and recycled stone.
  • Lighting — daylighting strategies, layered artificial light, and the technology behind great atmosphere.
  • Space Planning — circulation, proportion, and the human dimensions of rooms.
  • Furniture — timeless pieces, craftsmanship, and the ethics of buying well.
Principles

Our Values

Independence

We are not affiliated with any brand, developer, or institution. Our editorial decisions are made solely by our team.

Depth over speed

We take time to research, interview, and refine. Every article is a considered piece, not a news snippet.

Material honesty

We champion finishes and structures that age with grace, and we ask hard questions about sustainability.

Accessibility

Design writing should be clear, not elitist. We explain concepts without jargon and welcome curious readers.

Process

How We Work

Every article begins with a question. We then research widely, speak to practitioners, and visit projects wherever possible. Our writers work with editors through multiple drafts to ensure accuracy, clarity, and nuance.

We do not accept sponsored content disguised as editorial. When we partner with brands for advertising, it is clearly labelled and kept separate from our journalism. Our readers' trust is our most valuable asset.

If you have a story idea, a project you think deserves attention, or feedback on our work, we would love to hear from you. Visit our contact page to get in touch.
