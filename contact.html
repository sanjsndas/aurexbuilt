FAQ

Common questions

How do I pitch a story to Aurex? +

Send a concise pitch (200–300 words) outlining your idea, angle, and why it suits our audience. Include links to previous work if applicable. Email editors@aurexbuiltperspectives.com with "Pitch" in the subject line.

Do you accept sponsored content? +

We work with select partners on clearly labelled sponsored features and display advertising. We do not accept guest posts or link insertions. Contact partners@aurexbuiltperspectives.com for our media kit.

Can I republish an Aurex article? +

You may quote up to 150 words with a clear attribution and a link back to the original article. For full republication, please request permission first.

I have a correction or update. Who should I contact? +

Please email editors@aurexbuiltperspectives.com with the article URL and the specific correction. We review all corrections promptly.

About Aurex Built Perspectives

Aurex Built Perspectives is an independent editorial publication dedicated to architecture, interior design, and the built environment. We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and critical essays that help readers create spaces of lasting value. Our team is based in Milan, London, and New York.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Aurex Built Perspectives collects minimal data necessary to improve your reading experience. We do not sell or share personal information with third parties. Email addresses provided for newsletters are stored securely and used solely for communication from our editorial team. You may unsubscribe at any time.

Terms of Use

All content on this website is for informational purposes only. While we strive for accuracy, architectural and design decisions should always involve qualified professionals. Reproduction of articles without permission is prohibited. Excerpts may be used with proper attribution and a link back to the original article.

Cookie Policy

We use essential cookies to ensure basic site functionality and analytics cookies to understand how readers engage with our content. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. No personal data is shared with advertisers.
