Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 January 2025. This policy explains how Aurex Built Perspectives collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Aurex Built Perspectives ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website www.aurexbuiltperspectives.com. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our readers. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we may collect from you, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you voluntarily provide to us, and information automatically collected through your use of the site.

Type of Information Examples How We Collect
Personal Identifiers Name, email address Contact forms, newsletter sign-up
Correspondence Message content, story pitches Contact forms, direct email
Usage Data Pages visited, time on site, referring URL Analytics cookies (see our Cookies Policy)
Technical Data IP address, browser type, device type Server logs, analytics

We do not collect sensitive personal data (such as health information, financial data, or precise geolocation) and we do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 16.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries — When you contact us via our contact form or email, we use your name and email address to reply to your message.
  • To send our newsletter — If you subscribe to "The Weekly Perspective," we use your email address to deliver our newsletter. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.
  • To improve our content and website — We analyse aggregated usage data to understand which articles are most read and how readers navigate our site, so we can improve the editorial experience.
  • To manage editorial contributions — If you pitch a story or submit a correction, we use your correspondence to evaluate and respond to your submission.
  • To comply with legal obligations — In rare cases, we may need to retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for processing your personal data is:

  • Consent — for newsletter subscriptions and non-essential cookies.
  • Legitimate interest — for responding to inquiries, improving our website, and preventing fraud.
  • Legal obligation — for compliance with applicable laws.

4. Cookies and Analytics

We use a minimal set of cookies to ensure our website functions correctly and to understand how readers engage with our content. Essential cookies are required for basic site functionality. Analytics cookies are only set with your consent. For full details, please see our Cookies Policy.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — We use trusted third-party services for email delivery (e.g., newsletter platform) and website analytics. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the services they provide to us.
  • Legal requirements — We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.
  • Business transfers — If Aurex Built Perspectives is involved in a merger or acquisition, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy:

  • Newsletter subscriptions — Retained until you unsubscribe, plus a short period thereafter to honour your request.
  • Contact form inquiries — Retained for up to 24 months for reference and follow-up.
  • Analytics data — Retained in aggregated, anonymised form indefinitely. Raw log data is retained for up to 12 months.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of access — You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — You may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — You may request that we delete your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Right to restrict processing — You may request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability — You may request that we transfer your data to another organisation.
  • Right to object — You may object to our processing of your data based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@aurexbuiltperspectives.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Data Transfers

Our editorial team is based in Milan, London, and New York. Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own. When we transfer data outside the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (e.g., sources cited in articles, social media platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact us about privacy: If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please email privacy@aurexbuiltperspectives.com or write to us at: Aurex Built Perspectives, Via Brera 12, 20121 Milan, Italy.
